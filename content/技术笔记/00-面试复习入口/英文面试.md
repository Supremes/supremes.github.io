# 英文面试速记

> 原则：别编数据，别编规模，别编“全球千万用户”。把你真实做过的事情说清楚就够了。

## 1. 讲项目难点

### 可直接复述版

The most challenging project for me was my first major project. I ran into two main issues: memory leaks and concurrency problems.  
For the memory issue, I used profiling tools and read the relevant materials to understand the underlying allocation behavior. After that, I adjusted the strategy and fixed the leak.  
For the concurrency issue, I used Redis together with Lua scripts to implement an atomic compare-and-set style update, and combined it with retries to make sure the final data stayed correct.

### 面试官真正想听什么

- 你遇到了什么问题
- 你怎么定位
- 你怎么解决
- 你学到了什么

## 2. 为什么加入这家公司

I'd like to join because your values align with mine, and this role matches both my interests and my skill set. I believe I can contribute while continuing to grow here.

## 3. 未来几年的规划

In the next few years, I hope to keep improving my engineering skills, take on more ownership, and grow into someone who can solve more complex problems with better judgment.

## 4. 为什么离职

For long-term personal planning, I hope to ....

> 这类问题要克制，别抱怨前公司、别输出情绪。

## 5. 如何应对压力

I work well under pressure. When things get busy, I first prioritize the most important tasks, then break the work down and handle it step by step instead of panicking.

## 6. 你的优点

I have a strong sense of responsibility and a solid work ethic. Once I take ownership of something, I stay focused until it is done properly.

## 7. 一个更稳的答题框架

可以统一按这个顺序说：

1. Situation
2. Problem
3. Action
4. Result
5. Learning

这样比零散背句子更稳。
